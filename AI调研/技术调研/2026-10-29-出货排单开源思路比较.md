## 1. 比较结论

**本轮只借鉴“车次组织多项交付、安排与实际分别留结果、防重复与分批有边界”。不把仓库拣货批次等同你们的出货排单，不因项目能做路线优化就扩大需求。** 当前建议仍是人工表格排单、少量变化录入和有据回查。

## 2. 原始来源与具体限制

- [Delivery Trip官方文档](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/delivery-trip)说明车辆、司机、时间、多站点及关联交付；地图估时／优化需外部地图。只采车次聚合思路，不采其自动通知／地图或完整库存流程。
- [所查Delivery Trip源码](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/4d8a0664a9e2a0375025ac2685841ed05095f023/erpnext/stock/doctype/delivery_trip/delivery_trip.py)：validate、on_update、release_removed_delivery_notes、update_status为此次有限阅读重点，不冒称全仓库审核或运行通过；[许可证](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/4d8a0664a9e2a0375025ac2685841ed05095f023/license.txt)。
- [OCA creation manifest](https://github.com/OCA/stock-logistics-workflow/blob/328cc94c4bc9d6755d996c98a42dff77b74896b4/stock_picking_batch_creation/__manifest__.py)及[README](https://github.com/OCA/stock-logistics-workflow/blob/328cc94c4bc9d6755d996c98a42dff77b74896b4/stock_picking_batch_creation/README.rst)；[planner manifest](https://github.com/OCA/stock-logistics-workflow/blob/328cc94c4bc9d6755d996c98a42dff77b74896b4/stock_picking_batch_planner/__manifest__.py)及[Alpha说明](https://github.com/OCA/stock-logistics-workflow/blob/328cc94c4bc9d6755d996c98a42dff77b74896b4/stock_picking_batch_planner/README.rst)。没有据此核整个Odoo栈的运行要求／迁移或许可兼容性。
